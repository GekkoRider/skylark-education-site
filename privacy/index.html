Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Aqua Navis Ltd (trading as "Skylark Education", "we", "us") is the data controller for the personal information described here. This policy explains what we collect when you join Skylark Learning Club, why, and what rights you and your child have over it.

1. What we collect

DataFrom
Parent/carer name and email addressThe enrolment form and Stripe Checkout
Child's first name and year groupThe enrolment form
Billing address and payment detailsStripe Checkout — we never see or store full card details
Session recordings and attendanceGenerated when your child attends a live session
Emails and support messagesWhen you contact hello@skylark.education

2. Why we use it

  • To run your membership — matching your child to a cohort, running sessions, billing, and sending your monthly pack and progress reports (necessary to perform our contract with you).
  • For safeguarding — session recordings help us protect children in line with our safeguarding duties (legitimate interest).
  • To improve the service and respond to support requests (legitimate interest).
  • Where the law requires it — for example, keeping financial records for tax purposes.

3. Children's information

We only collect the minimum information needed about your child — their first name and year group — and never ask a child to provide information directly online themselves. A parent or carer is always the account holder and point of contact.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted processors to run the service:

  • Stripe — for payment processing and billing. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to the payment details you give them directly.
  • Google Workspace — for email and our online classroom.
  • GitHub Pages — for hosting this website.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone else without telling you, except where required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep membership and safeguarding records for as long as your child is enrolled and for a reasonable period afterwards (typically up to 7 years) to meet our legal and safeguarding obligations. Financial records are kept as required by UK tax law.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • show you the personal data we hold about you or your child;
  • correct anything that's inaccurate;
  • delete your data, where we're not required to keep it;
  • restrict or object to certain uses of your data; and
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these, email hello@skylark.education. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

7. Cookies

This website doesn't set its own tracking cookies. When you go through checkout, Stripe may set cookies necessary to process your payment securely — see Stripe's privacy policy for details.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a material change, we'll email you before it takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email hello@skylark.education.
