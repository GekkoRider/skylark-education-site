Questions parents ask

The things worth knowing before you join

What age groups do you cater for?
We run sessions for KS1 (Years 1–2) and KS2 (Years 3–6), with a dedicated 11+ preparation strand for pupils in Years 5–6.
What if my child misses a session?
Every session is recorded and shared with your family within 24 hours, so your child never falls behind.
Can I cancel?
Yes — any time, with no penalty. We just ask for 30 days' notice so we can manage cohort places fairly for other families.
How does payment work?
Membership is £99 a month, billed automatically through Stripe from the day your child's cohort is confirmed. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout — we never see or store them.
What technology do we need?
Any device with a camera, microphone and internet connection. We use a simple, child-friendly online classroom — there's no technical setup.
How are tutors chosen?
Every Skylark tutor is qualified, DBS-checked and trained on our curriculum before they lead a cohort. You'll meet yours in the first session.
